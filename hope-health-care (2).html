Questions patients ask

Where is the clinic?

Pazhakulam Medical Centre is at Tempo Stand, opposite Pazhakulam, in the Pazhakulam, Pallickal and Nooranad area, Pathanamthitta district. Use the Get directions button in the contact section.

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day, Monday to Sunday, from 9:00 am to 10:00 pm.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking saves you waiting time, but we also see walk-in patients as doctors are free.

Is the WhatsApp reminder service free?

Yes. Reminders and care tips are included with every booking. Reply STOP any time to opt out.

Can I get my reports on WhatsApp?

Yes. Lab reports are shared as a secure PDF to the number you booked with.

How is my health data protected?

We share only what you need to know and never post medical details publicly.
